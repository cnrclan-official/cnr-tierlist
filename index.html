How to apply

  1. Open Blockman Go and search for the account ID above to find the account.
  2. Send a friend request with a message that includes all the details listed.
  3. Wait for a tester to reply and schedule your tier test. Your tier is decided only after the test.
Beware of imitators: only this account ID handles applications. Nobody from CNR will ask for your password or payment.
